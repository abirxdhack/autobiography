Chapter 05 · Unseen Struggles

The Battles Nobody Sees

"Some battles are fought without an audience, without applause, and without anyone knowing how difficult they are."

  • Health
  • Faith
  • Family
  • Hope
Start Reading
01

Too much, too soon

I never imagined that, at just seventeen, I would already have to deal with so many challenges concerning my health and well-being. Hair loss, back pain, swollen lymph nodes, recurring allergies, episodes of fever, and emotional struggles have become concerns that I never expected to face so early in life.

02

Behind an ordinary smile

From the outside, I may appear perfectly fine. I can smile, laugh, communicate with others, pursue my interests, and continue with my everyday responsibilities. But behind that ordinary appearance, there are struggles I rarely talk about. Sometimes, the physical exhaustion becomes difficult to ignore. At other times, it is the frustration, unexplained mood changes, uncertainty, and thoughts that I keep to myself.

03

The heaviest thought

Perhaps one of the heaviest thoughts I carry is the fear of not being able to accomplish everything my parents dream of for me. Their hopes, sacrifices, and expectations mean more to me than I can easily express. I want to build a meaningful future, achieve something worthwhile, and give them reasons to feel proud of the person they raised. Yet, sometimes, I wonder whether I will have the strength, health, and time to fulfil everything I have imagined.

Above everything else, I hope to live long enough, stay strong enough, and achieve enough to make my parents genuinely proud.

Abir

04

Not for sympathy

I do not share these experiences to seek sympathy or make anyone feel sorry for me. I simply believe that these struggles are also a part of my story. They have taught me that life does not always move according to our plans, and that even people who appear completely normal may be carrying burdens that others know nothing about.

05

Faith as my strength

Through all of this, my faith remains an important source of hope. I turn to Allah and ask Him for strength when I feel weak, patience when things become difficult, healing from what troubles me, and guidance for the future I am trying to build.

06

Still moving forward

I do not know exactly what lies ahead. I do not have answers to every question, and I cannot predict how everything will unfold. But I still have dreams, responsibilities, and reasons to keep moving forward.

Above everything else, I hope to live long enough, stay strong enough, and achieve enough to make my parents genuinely proud.

07

Holding on to hope

Some battles are fought without an audience, without applause, and without anyone knowing how difficult they are. Mine is one of them. And despite everything, I am still holding on to hope.

And despite everything, I am still holding on to hope.
