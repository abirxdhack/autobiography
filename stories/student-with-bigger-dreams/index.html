The Student With Bigger Dreams

01

More than grades

Being a student has always been an important part of my life, but I have never wanted my identity to be limited to academic achievements alone. I believe education is not just about textbooks, examinations, or grades; it is also about discovering my abilities, developing new skills, and understanding the world beyond the classroom.

02

Beyond the syllabus

Currently, I am pursuing my higher secondary education as an HSC 2027 student at Government Yeasin College. Like every student, I have academic responsibilities and challenges, but alongside my regular studies, I have developed a strong curiosity about technology, programming, science, and other areas of knowledge.

I enjoy exploring things beyond my academic syllabus. Whenever something catches my attention, I feel motivated to understand how it works and how I can use that knowledge in real life. I believe practical experience and independent learning are just as important as theoretical education.

03

Not always easy

I know that the journey of a student is not always easy. There are examinations, responsibilities, uncertainties, and moments when balancing different interests becomes challenging. Nevertheless, I want to make the most of my student life by learning, experimenting, creating, and improving myself.

I do not consider myself someone who has already achieved everything. I am still learning, making mistakes, discovering my strengths, and working towards my goals. But I have ambitions that extend far beyond the boundaries of a classroom.

I want to gain knowledge, develop abilities, and become someone capable of turning ideas into reality.

Abir

04

What being a student means

For me, being a student means remaining curious, having the courage to explore unfamiliar subjects, and never stopping the process of learning.

I don't just want to earn a certificate at the end of my education. I want to gain knowledge, develop abilities, and become someone capable of turning ideas into reality.
